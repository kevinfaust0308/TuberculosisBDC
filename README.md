# STEM Fellowship Big Data Challenge 

## What can data science tell us about past, current, and future patterns of infection?

### Abstract
This paper took a multidisciplinary approach of combining the fields of bacteriology, virology
and epidemiology. We assessed the epidemiological consequences of both Human
Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) and Tuberculosis (TB) using graphical representations and
various statistical analyses through Python 3.5 programming via the utilization of datasets
about the worldwide occurrence of TB and HIV. Granted this, it was then hypothesized that
HIV increases the chance of an individual getting TB and extrapulmonary TB (EPTB), thus
increasing affected individuals’ chance of dying. Furthermore, socio-economic conditions of
various nations were surveyed to determine possible risk factors.
